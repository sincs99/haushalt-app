## 2. Data we process

### Account

- E-mail address, display name, password (stored only as a bcrypt hash)
- Time of registration, e-mail confirmation and acceptance of these terms
- Sign-in sessions (hashed refresh tokens) to detect abuse

### Household data

Everything you and the other members of your household enter in the app: shopping lists, tasks, chore schedule, expenses and settlements, budgets, events and polls, meal planning and recipes, pets and plants, notes, uploaded documents and photos, and NFC/QR tags. This data is visible to all members of the same household.

### Technical data

- Server logs with IP address, time, requested address and browser identifier (operation, troubleshooting, abuse protection; short retention)
- Rate-limit counters per IP address (volatile)

## 3. What we use the data for

- Providing the app and syncing between the devices of your household (performance of contract)
- Account security e-mails: address confirmation, password reset, notice after a password change (no advertising, no newsletter)
- Abuse protection and service security (legitimate interest)
- Billing of paid plans if you subscribe to one (performance of contract, statutory retention duties)

## 4. Cookies and local storage

The app uses **no tracking** and no advertising cookies. A single, strictly necessary cookie (`casa_rt`) is set for signing in. Your device's browser storage holds settings such as language, selected household and cached content for offline use.

## 5. Push notifications

Only if you enable them on a device. A device address is then stored with the push service of your browser or operating system (e.g. Apple, Google, Mozilla). Messages contain short reminder texts. You can disable push at any time in the settings.

## 6. AI assistant (optional)

The AI assistant (recipe suggestions, plant care) must be explicitly enabled by a household admin. Only then are the respective inputs (e.g. ingredient list, plant species) sent to the API of Anthropic PBC (USA). No account data and no other household data is sent. According to Anthropic, API inputs are not used to train its models.

## 7. Payments (optional)

Paid plans are processed via Stripe Payments Europe Ltd. (Ireland). Payment details (card, bank account) are processed exclusively by Stripe; we only receive the subscription status, the customer number and invoice details. Stripe's privacy policy applies in addition.

## 8. Disclosure to third parties

Apart from the service providers mentioned (hosting, e-mail delivery, push services, Anthropic when the AI assistant is enabled, Stripe for payments, optionally an error monitoring without personal data) we do not disclose data – unless legally required to do so.

## 9. Retention

- Account and household data: as long as your account or the household exists
- After deleting your account: your account data is anonymised immediately. Expenses and settlements that concern other members remain visible to them as "former member" without reference to your name.
- When the last member leaves, the household with all data and files is deleted.
- Server logs: at most 30 days; invoice data according to statutory periods
- Backups are overwritten regularly.

## 10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You change or delete your account and password yourself in the settings. For anything else, write to {{operator.email}}. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority.

## 11. Data security

Transmission exclusively encrypted (TLS), passwords as bcrypt hashes, access to household data only for members, regular security updates. Uploaded files are validated and only retrievable when signed in.

## 12. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the legal situation changes. The current version is available at any time in the app under "Privacy".
